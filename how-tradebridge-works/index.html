Evidence states

What is known matters as much as what isn't.

We preserve the distinction between claims and substantiated facts.

VERIFIED

Source-checked and supported narrowly.

COMPANY-REPORTED

Attributed to the company rather than independently confirmed.

INFERENCE

A qualified interpretation, not a settled fact.

UNKNOWN

Unverified or not yet answered; do not guess.

CONFLICTED

Competing evidence remains unresolved.

KILL CASE

The evidence supports holding or declining rather than proceeding.

Accountable transitions

Human authorization remains explicit.

Signals can justify investigation. They do not establish budget, purchase intent, capacity, investment fit or authority to act.

Opportunity Desk

Initial commercially meaningful inbound is assessed for evidence, scope and fit. Non-fit and unknown states are acceptable conclusions.

Conversion Desk

Direct pursuit follows only after accepted qualification and a controlled handoff. Growth can nurture non-immediate interest.

Scope can change. Standard cannot. Consequential operating, commercial, financial, regulated and ownership decisions require approved human authority.
