How work is performed

From criteria to a decision brief.

We test supporting and contradictory evidence before treating a signal as opportunity.

Define

Criteria, exclusions, scope and the decision to be made.

Investigate

Gather sources, compare facts, expose conflicts and keep unknowns visible.

Decide

Public-safe evidence

The work is not a lead-volume promise.

TradeBridge prioritizes evidence quality and defensible fit over claims of guaranteed results.

Methodology

What we can describe

  • Traceable source and qualification criteria
  • Documented fit/exclusion reasons and counterevidence
  • An explicit distinction between verified and unverified findings

What we do not claim

  • Guaranteed sales or generic lead volume
  • Invented contact or market certainty
  • Legal, engineering or regulated technical conclusions outside the engagement scope
