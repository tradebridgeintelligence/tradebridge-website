How TradeBridge works

Start with the decision. Investigate both sides.

The objective is not to generate information for its own sake. The objective is to make a specific commercial decision more defensible.

STEP 01

Define the decision

We establish what management actually needs to decide, what is already known, what remains uncertain, and what evidence would materially change the choice.

STEP 02

Investigate both sides

We look for evidence supporting the opportunity and evidence against it. Weak evidence does not become strong evidence because it supports the desired answer.

STEP 03

Make it defensible

We separate what is verified, what remains unknown, what deserves pursuit, what should be rejected, and what must be verified before resources are committed.

Our standard

Intelligence We Can Defend.

TradeBridge is built around evidence discipline rather than confident-sounding conclusions.

Evidence first. Claims should be tied to evidence that can be examined, challenged, and traced.
Unknown stays unknown. When the evidence does not establish something, we do not fill the gap with a guess.
Negative evidence matters. A reason not to pursue an opportunity can be as valuable as a reason to pursue it.
Signal is not opportunity. A promising event, announcement, expansion, or trend does not automatically create a worthwhile commercial move.
Know the boundary. When the real problem belongs with another specialist, TradeBridge should stop.

When TradeBridge may begin

When another professional reaches the point where the unresolved question becomes which market, customer, supplier, distributor, partner, channel, geography, or commercial opportunity deserves pursuit, that may be our lane.
