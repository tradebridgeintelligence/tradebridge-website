How TradeBridge works

Start with the decision. Investigate both sides.

The objective is not to generate information for its own sake. The objective is to make a specific commercial decision more defensible.

01

Define the decision

Establish what management actually needs to decide, what is known, what remains uncertain, and what evidence would materially change the choice.

02

Investigate for and against

Look for supporting evidence and counterevidence. A signal does not become an opportunity merely because it supports the desired answer.

03

Make the recommendation defensible

Separate what is verified, what remains unknown, what deserves pursuit, what should be rejected, and what must be verified before commitment.

Our standard

Evidence. Objectivity. Clarity.

Different questions. Same standard. We apply a rigorous, transparent process so management can make commercial decisions with confidence.

About TradeBridge

TradeBridge Commercial Intelligence was built for established businesses facing important outside commercial decisions without necessarily having a dedicated internal commercial-intelligence team.

Founder Joshua Nichols works directly on engagements using structured research, source verification, counterevidence, explicit uncertainty controls, and decision-focused analysis.

TradeBridge is not automatically the technical expert in a client's industry. Our responsibility is the commercial intelligence surrounding the decision — and knowing when the question belongs with somebody else.
