Venture Thesis

What merits selective ownership?

A process-oriented lens for deciding when ownership is preferable to ordinary paid work.

Ownership should not be the default outcome of providing research, technology or operating expertise. The decision depends on the repeatability of need, evidence of economics, capacity to improve execution and the alignment of incentives.

Test the cash-equivalent case

Would TradeBridge pay the cash-equivalent value of its contribution to receive the proposed upside? If not, a paid engagement or a decline may be more appropriate.

Distinguish four paths

BUILD, PARTNER, INVEST and ACQUIRE describe possibilities, not standard offers. Each requires a different analysis of governance, economics, diligence and downside.

No commitment without review

A website inquiry does not establish an investment offer, an ownership allocation or a commitment. Specific arrangements, if any, require separate review, authorization and documentation.
