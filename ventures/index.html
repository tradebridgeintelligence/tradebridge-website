Public model

BUILD. PARTNER. INVEST. ACQUIRE.

Four paths may be considered where repeated evidence and operating economics justify them.

BUILD

Incubate owned products or businesses when repeated evidence supports a real market problem.

PARTNER

Work with selected companies when TradeBridge capabilities may materially improve execution.

INVEST

Consider ownership where diligence, economics, alignment and governance support a decision.

ACQUIRE

Evaluate buying rather than building when ownership and operating economics support it.

Screening discipline

Would we pay cash-equivalent value for the work?

The same value test applied to cash investment should govern decisions about services exchanged for potential ownership.

We investigate

  • The problem, customer and repeatability of demand
  • Unit economics and path to defensible value
  • Our ability to contribute beyond capital
  • Governance, alignment and downside
Selective is not automatic.

If an opportunity does not support strategic ownership, it may be better served by ordinary paid services or declined.

Ventures disclosures

A process for evaluation, not an investment offer.

TradeBridge Ventures describes a disciplined ownership model; individual opportunities remain subject to review.

Sending an email or other inquiry does not create a commitment, investment offer, equity entitlement or partnership. TradeBridge does not represent that it operates an investment fund, has committed investment capital, a closed portfolio, realized returns or exits. Any ownership arrangement would require separate diligence, terms, approvals and documentation.
