Privacy

How website information is used.

TradeBridge Intelligence uses information you choose to provide to assess and respond to business inquiries. This page describes website data handling, limited referral attribution, and how to contact us about information you submitted.

Contact usHow TradeBridge Works
Website inquiries

Information provided through forms.

An inquiry may include your name, work email, company, website, role, the description and timing of your request, and explicit permission to contact you about that inquiry. Relevant first-party campaign, referral and landing-page details may accompany a submitted request.

The website sends a submitted inquiry to a receiving service that must confirm its record before the website displays a successful submission. Inquiries are initially reviewed through TradeBridge's internal Opportunity Desk. Submission does not establish a client relationship, guarantee acceptance of work, or constitute an investment offer or commitment. Do not submit controlled drawings, financial account information, passwords, regulated sensitive records or confidential third-party materials.

Service providers and safeguards

Our inquiry systems use Render for website receiving and data storage, Cloudflare Turnstile to help limit automated submissions, and Google Workspace for authorized internal notifications. These providers may process information necessary to support those functions. A security challenge may involve device or interaction signals; it is not a guarantee that all automated activity will be blocked.

Attribution and marketing

The initial website uses first-party browser session storage for limited campaign and referral attribution during your visit. Relevant campaign, referral and landing-page details may be included with your inquiry. The initial release does not offer newsletter enrollment or optional Google Analytics 4 measurement. Submitting an inquiry does not enroll you in marketing communications.

Retention and information requests

Inquiry information, consent records and related operating records may be kept for different periods depending on the status of the request, business needs and applicable obligations. Separate copies can exist in security logs, operational notification emails and provider-managed database recovery systems. Removing information from an active record does not necessarily remove it immediately from every backup or external copy.

To ask about personal information submitted through this website, including an access, correction or deletion request, contact jnichols@tradebridgeintelligence.com. We may need to verify your identity or authority to make the request and assess applicable legal obligations or exceptions before taking action.
