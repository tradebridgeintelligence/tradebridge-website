Privacy

How website information is used.

TradeBridge uses the information you choose to provide to evaluate and respond to inquiries. This page summarizes current website handling.

Contact usHow TradeBridge Works
Website inquiries

Information provided through forms.

An inquiry may include your name, work email, company, website, role, your description of the issue, timing and separately chosen newsletter permission. Campaign parameters and first landing page help attribute an inquiry to its source.

TradeBridge's receiving systems and policies must be reviewed before launch. Until an approved receiver is configured, the web form does not acknowledge a successful submission. Do not submit controlled drawings, financial account information, credentials or regulated sensitive records.

Optional measurement

Analytics tracking is disabled unless you explicitly allow it in Measurement settings and a configured measurement account exists. The website uses browser session storage to retain attribution during your visit and local storage for optional analytics preferences.

Contact and changes

To ask about information you submitted or request changes, contact jnichols@tradebridgeintelligence.com. This is an operational draft, subject to privacy/legal review before public launch.
