How website and email contact information is handled.

Email contact and hosting

What information may be processed.

Contact is initiated through your own email application, not by submitting a form on this website.

Direct email

If you choose to email us, your email provider sends your message and associated address, routing information, subject and any content you include to TradeBridge's Google Workspace mailbox. TradeBridge may use that information to evaluate your business inquiry, respond when appropriate, and maintain related records. An email does not establish an engagement, promise a response, or constitute an investment offer or commitment.

Do not email passwords, banking or financial-account credentials, regulated records, controlled documents, sensitive proprietary materials or confidential third-party information.

Website hosting and browser information

Our website hosting provider may process basic connection information, such as IP addresses, request timing, URLs and security logs, to serve and protect the site. The website-first page scripts do not store marketing attribution in browser session storage or send inquiry data to our intake API. This version has no Cloudflare inquiry challenge, newsletter signup or optional GA4 analytics. Provider-level security and operational logging may still occur.

Retention and information requests

Email correspondence and associated administrative or security records may be retained according to business needs and applicable obligations. Separate copies can exist in mail systems, logs and provider-managed backups. Deleting or changing one copy does not automatically remove all others, and exceptions may apply where preservation is legally required.

To ask about personal information you have emailed to TradeBridge, including access, correction or deletion requests, contact jnichols@tradebridgeintelligence.com. We may need to verify your identity or authority and review applicable obligations before responding. Requests will be evaluated under applicable legal requirements and exceptions; this notice does not promise immediate deletion from every system or backup.
